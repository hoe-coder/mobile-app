# Campus Lost & Found: walkthrough

This walkthrough shows how a student can browse campus lost-and-found posts, manage their own items, and respond when someone may have found a belonging.

## 1. Review the introduction

Move through the introductory screens to see the app’s main tasks: report a lost or found item, search campus listings, and get an item back by contacting its owner. Select **Start using Campus Lost & Found** to continue.

## 2. Browse the campus feed

The Home screen shows recent posts from campus. Scroll through the listings to review item photos, descriptions, and locations. Use the search field or category filters to narrow the results.

## 3. Open a listing

Select a post to review its details and decide whether it matches the item you lost or found. Use the available listing action to contact the poster or mark the item as relevant to you.

## 4. Review your items

Open **My Items** to see the items associated with your account. The screen groups posts by their status, including active, returned, and saved items. Review or manage a post from its item card.

## 5. Check inbox requests

Open **Inbox** to review messages and requests related to your listings. Read the item and location details in a request to determine whether the person has identified the correct item.

## 6. Respond to a return request

For a request that matches your item, choose **Accept** to continue arranging its return. Choose **Decline** if the request does not match or you cannot verify the claim.

## 7. Complete the handoff

Use the conversation with the requester to agree on a safe, convenient campus handoff. Update the item’s status once it has been returned.
